Study Buddy

Highlight anything on a page and ask about it.

Provider & key

Any endpoint that speaks the Anthropic Messages API. The extension appends /v1/messages.

A key created for one specific workspace needs nothing here. Otherwise copy the ID column at Settings → Workspaces (pasting the whole console URL works too).

Model

How hard the model thinks before answering. Lower is faster and cheaper.

Show the model's reasoning

Streams a summary of the thinking above each answer. Ignored on Haiku.

Retry declined requests on a fallback model

Server-side fallback for Opus 5. Turn off if your account isn't enrolled in the beta.

Reading

Applies to the panel on pages, the popup, notes, settings and the PDF viewer.

Show the quick-action bubble on selection

Turn off to use only the keyboard shortcut and right-click menu.

Highlight passages I ask about

Highlights come back when you revisit the page. Click one to reopen it with its conversation, Alt-click to remove it.

Save every answer to my study notes

Answers land in your notes as they arrive, so closing the page loses nothing. Click “Saved ✓” under an answer to take it back out. Turn off to save only the ones you pick with “Save note”.

Open PDFs in the study viewer

Chrome's built-in PDF viewer has no selectable text for an extension to reach. This opens PDFs in the extension's own viewer instead, where highlighting works like any other page. Local files also need "Allow access to file URLs" on the extensions page.

Characters of nearby page text sent along so the model can disambiguate. 0 sends the passage alone.

Shortcuts: ⌘⇧E explain the selection · ⌘⇧U show/hide the panel · Esc close. Change them at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
